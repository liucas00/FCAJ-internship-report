###  [Blog 1 - 7 IAM Best Practices for a More Secure AWS Account](3.1-Blog1/)
Explore 7 essential IAM principles that help secure your AWS Account. This covers crucial practices ranging from minimizing Root User access, enabling MFA, and applying the Least Privilege principle, to utilizing IAM Roles and CloudTrail. These are fundamental yet vital best practices for any system running on AWS.

###  [Blog 2 - What I love most is that Auto Scaling isn't just for massive systems.](3.2-Blog2/)
Auto Scaling is not exclusively for systems with millions of users. This article discusses how Amazon ECS Service Auto Scaling enables your application to automatically scale resources up or down based on demand. This helps optimize costs and improves operational capabilities on AWS.

###  [Blog 3 - Managing Sensitive Information on AWS: When the .env file is no longer the optimal choice](3.3-Blog3/)
