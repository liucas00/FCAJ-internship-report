### General Evaluation

#### 1. Working Environment

Throughout the program, I found the learning environment to be friendly, supportive, and collaborative. Mentors and community members were always willing to help whenever I encountered difficulties. The workshops and knowledge-sharing sessions also provided valuable opportunities to explore AWS services and cloud technologies in a practical way.

#### 2. Mentor and Admin Support

The mentors provided clear guidance and encouraged me to think independently instead of simply giving direct answers. This approach helped me develop better problem-solving skills. The admin team was also responsive and supportive whenever I needed assistance with AWS accounts or learning materials.

#### 3. Relevance to My Learning Goals

The program aligned well with my interest in Cloud Computing and DevOps. Through hands-on workshops, I gained experience with AWS services such as IAM, Amazon ECS, Lambda, CloudWatch, Systems Manager, and Secrets Manager instead of only learning theoretical concepts.

#### 4. Learning and Skill Development

Besides learning how to use AWS services, I also improved my understanding of cloud architecture, deployment practices, and AWS security principles. The program also strengthened my self-learning ability, technical reading skills, and problem-solving mindset.

#### 5. Community Culture

One of the things I appreciated most was the collaborative culture within the FCAJ community. Everyone was willing to share knowledge and support each other, creating a positive learning environment that motivated me to continue exploring AWS.

#### 6. Overall Experience

Overall, this program provided valuable practical experience. The knowledge I gained not only supported my university studies but also gave me a better understanding of how cloud applications are designed and deployed in real-world environments.

---

### Additional Questions

#### What did you enjoy the most during the program?

The hands-on workshops were the most valuable part of the program. Building and deploying AWS services by myself helped me understand how different cloud services work together in a real application.

#### What could be improved?

I would like to see more advanced workshops covering cloud architecture, cost optimization, and real-world business scenarios. More mini projects or case studies would also provide additional opportunities to apply what participants have learned.

#### Would you recommend this program to others?

Yes. I would definitely recommend it to students and beginners who want to learn AWS because the learning path is well structured, supported by experienced mentors, and focused on practical hands-on experience.

---

### Suggestions

I hope the program continues to expand with more advanced AWS workshops and experience-sharing sessions from industry professionals. If given the opportunity, I would be happy to continue participating in future activities to further improve my cloud knowledge and practical skills.
